📐 MATHEMATICAL RIGOR

Algorithmic Formulation & Model Architecture

Understanding the quantitative foundations powering JUDETIPS predictions.

1. 20-Site Global Consensus Meta-Model

Every fixture is evaluated across 20 global prediction authorities (Forebet, PredictZ, WinDrawWin, WhoScored, Opta Analyst, SofaScore, Vitibet, Statarea, etc.). The collective vote distribution eliminates single-model blindspots and prevents artificial 1-1 draw convergence.

2. Dixon-Coles Bivariate Poisson Modeling

Standard Poisson models underestimate low-scoring draws and asymmetric results. The Dixon-Coles adjustment applies a calibrated covariance parameter ρ = -0.04 to realistic expected goal differentials.

3. Corner & Disciplinary Market Engine

Corner distributions are derived through team shot-volume ratings, territorial attacking dominance, and tempo interaction functions to generate Over/Under 8.5, 9.5, and 10.5 corner probabilities.

4. Fractional Kelly Criterion Staking

To prevent bankroll ruin while optimizing long-term geometric compounding, JUDETIPS recommends Quarter-Kelly staking ($f^* = \frac{bp - q}{4b}$).
