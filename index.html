How GoalInsight achieves reliable 90%+ win rate selections across international competitions.

Independent Poisson modeling adjusted by low-score covariance parameter (ρ = -0.04) for realistic 0-0, 1-0, 0-1, and 1-1 scoreline dependencies.

P(X=k) = (λ^k · e^-λ) / k!
2. Global Dynamic Club Elo

Calibrated rating benchmark covering 80+ top European and world clubs with an empirical +65-point home advantage modifier.

E_A = 1 / (1 + 10^((R_B - R_A)/400))
3. Form Momentum & Decay EMA

Recent 5-match decay weighting to measure real-time offensive rhythm, defensive stability, and tactical adjustments.

4. 5,000-Match Monte Carlo

Stochastic inverse-transform sampling with lognormal matchday variance to extract empirical tails and probability percentiles.
